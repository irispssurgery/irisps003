# [[Ullift]]

## 📌 Brief Summary

Ullift is a next-generation High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (HIFU) lifting device that utilizes a dual-mode system (Linear and Dot) to target multiple dermal layers for skin tightening and contouring. It is designed to provide faster, more comfortable treatments with minimal downtime by stimulating multilayer collagen regeneration.

## 📖 Core Content

Ullift integrates several advanced technologies and features to optimize both clinical efficacy and user experience:

- **Dual-Mode HIFU System**: The device combines two distinct energy delivery methods in a single session:
    
    - **Linear Mode**: Uses a 1.5 mm cartridge to deliver continuous energy lines to the superficial dermal layer, enhancing collagen neogenesis and fibrosis modulation.
        
    - **Dot Mode**: Utilizes 3.0 mm, 4.5 mm, and 6.0 mm cartridges to deliver higher-intensity energy to deep dermal and SMAS layers for long-lasting lifting.
        
- **Treatment Versatility**: It includes five cartridge depths (1.5 mm, 3.0 mm, 4.5 mm, 6.0 mm, and 9.0 mm) suitable for various areas including the forehead, eyes, jawline, neck, and body.
    
- **Enhanced Comfort & Speed**: Features an ultra-fast scanning speed of up to 0.5 seconds per shot and smooth targeting to significantly reduce the pain often associated with traditional HIFU.
    
- **Intuitive Interface**: Equipped with a 12.1-inch high-resolution LCD and a simplified Graphical User Interface (GUI) designed to reduce user error and streamline clinical workflow.
    
- **Technical Specifications**:
    
    - **Type**: High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (HIFU).
        
    - **Handpieces**: Line-type dual handpieces.
        
    - **Frequency**: 4 MHz and 7 MHz.
        
    - **Interface**: 12.1-inch 1280*800 TFT LCD.
        
    - **Manufacturer**: Aestheon Co., Ltd., based in Busan, Republic of Korea.
